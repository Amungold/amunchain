# AmunChain — Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We, as contributors and maintainers of AmunChain, pledge to uphold
the constitutional principles of this project: determinism, sovereignty,
verifiability, and integrity in every interaction.

## Standards

**Expected behavior:**
- Respect the constitutional lineage and genesis identity.
- Communicate with precision — this is a formal protocol project.
- Assume good faith in technical discussions.
- Focus on architectural correctness and constitutional compliance.

**Unacceptable behavior:**
- Attempting to forge, tamper with, or misrepresent constitutional artifacts.
- Introducing non-deterministic code paths.
- Harassment, personal attacks, or trolling.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all AmunChain community spaces:
GitHub repositories, discussion forums, and any official communication
channels associated with the project.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive or unacceptable behavior may be reported to:

**conduct@amungold.global**

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated. Reports will remain
confidential to the extent possible.
