**Date:** 2026-05-31
**Status:** Draft – From Geometry to Reduction

## The Question of V7-003

> Can CCS be formulated as a reduction system, where the central
> property is not merging or selection, but **reduction** to a unique
> constitutional normal form?

V7-002 attempted to prove finality from graph geometry (rooted,
well-founded, confluent). But AmunChain experiments (V3-006B,
V3-007A) show that conflicting branches do not merge — one branch
dominates and the other becomes irrelevant. This is **reduction**,
not merging.

V7-003 reformulates CCS as a **Constitutional Reduction System.**

---

## 1. The Primitive Relation: Constitutional Reduction (⇒_C)

`C ⇒_C C'`

means: "Context `C` constitutionally reduces to context `C'`."

`C'` is the unique legitimate successor of `C`.
The constitution does not offer choices; it prescribes the next step.

---

## 2. Axioms of Constitutional Reduction

### Axiom R1: Foundational Root
`P₀ ⇒_C P₀`

The genesis context reduces to itself. It is the fixed point of
constitutional origin.

### Axiom R2: Deterministic Reduction
`∀ C : ∃! C' : C ⇒_C C'`

For every context, there is exactly one constitutional successor.
The future is not selected from alternatives; it is determined by
the reduction rules.

### Axiom R3: Unique Normal Form (Church-Rosser for CCS)
`∀ C : ∃! Λ_C : C ⇒*_C Λ_C ∧ NormalForm(Λ_C)`

Every context reduces to a unique constitutional normal form.
`Λ_C` is the final, irreducible constitutional reality.

### Axiom R4: Reachability
`∀ C : P₀ ⇒*_C C`

Every legitimate context is reachable from genesis.

---

## 3. Deriving All Previous Concepts

### Finality
`Final(C)` iff `NormalForm(C)` — no further reduction is possible.

### Resolution
Resolution is not a separate function. It is the reduction process
`⇒_C` itself. Competing paths are not merged; they are eliminated
by the determinism of `⇒_C`.

### Continuity
Continuity is the reduction chain from `P₀` to `Λ`.

### Conservation
Every legitimate context is reachable from `P₀` (R4).

### Monotonicity
Reduction never regresses; it advances toward the normal form.

### Authority
Authority is the unique normal form `Λ`.

## 4. Experimental Consistency

- **V3-006B (Conflicting QCs):** One QC reduces; the other does not.
  `⇒_C` selects exactly one.
- **V3-007A (Amendment forks):** One amendment reduces; competing
  proposals become dead branches.
- **V3-007B/C (Epoch transitions):** Reduction advances the normal form.
- **V3-007D (Recovery):** Reconstruction of the reduction chain from
  partial evidence.

All experiments support the reduction model over the merge model.

CCS is a **Constitutional Reduction System.**

The constitution is not a set of permissions.

possibilities into a single reality.

The fundamental structure of CCS is:
`(Contexts, ⇒_C, P₀, Λ)`

A deterministic reduction system with a unique normal form.
