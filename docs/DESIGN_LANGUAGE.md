# AMUN DESIGN PLATFORM

# DESIGN LANGUAGE

Version: 1.0

Status: FROZEN

Effective Date: 2026-07-01

---

# PURPOSE

This document defines the visual philosophy,
interaction principles,
and design language of the
Amun Design Platform.

Every official product SHALL follow
these rules.

---

# DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

The Amun Design Platform is built around
five core values.

- Clarity
- Trust
- Precision
- Consistency
- Longevity

Interfaces SHALL prioritize understanding
over decoration.

Visual elements exist to communicate meaning,
not to attract unnecessary attention.

---

# BRAND IDENTITY

The platform represents:

- Constitutional integrity
- Technical excellence
- Enterprise quality
- Scientific rigor
- Long-term stability

Every interface SHALL reflect these values.

---

# COLOR PHILOSOPHY

Primary Black

Represents:

- Foundation
- Stability
- Focus

---

Royal Gold

Represents:

- Trust
- Authority
- Value
- Amun identity

Gold SHALL be used sparingly
to emphasize important information.

---

Emerald

Represents:

- Health
- Success
- Constitutional validity
- Positive state

---

Blue

Represents:

- Information
- Connectivity
- Neutral actions

---

Red

Represents:

- Errors
- Security alerts
- Critical failures

Red SHALL NEVER be used
for decorative purposes.

---

# TYPOGRAPHY

Primary Font

Inter

Used for:

- Titles
- Body text
- Navigation
- Labels

---

Monospace Font

JetBrains Mono

Used for:

- Hashes
- Addresses
- Heights
- IDs
- Technical values
- Code

---

# SPACING

Whitespace SHALL be generous.

Interfaces SHALL breathe.

Crowded layouts are prohibited.

Spacing SHALL follow
Design Tokens only.

No arbitrary spacing values.

---

# BORDERS

Borders SHALL be subtle.

Primary separators SHALL use
low-contrast colors.

Gold borders SHALL indicate:

- Constitutional information
- Verified data
- Important protocol objects

---

# SHADOWS

Shadows SHALL communicate depth,
not decoration.

Heavy shadows are prohibited.

Glass effects SHALL remain subtle.

---

# MOTION

Animations SHALL communicate state.

Animations SHALL NEVER exist
for entertainment.

Recommended durations:

Fast

150ms

Normal

200ms

Slow

400ms

---

# ICONOGRAPHY

Icons SHALL be:

- Simple
- Consistent
- Geometric
- Readable

Icons SHALL be SVG only.

Raster icons are prohibited.

---

# DATA VISUALIZATION

Charts SHALL emphasize:

- Accuracy
- Readability
- Comparability

Decoration SHALL be minimal.

Every chart SHALL include:

- Labels
- Units
- Legend when necessary

---

# COMPONENT STYLE

Components SHALL be:

- Predictable
- Accessible
- Reusable
- Stateless where practical

Components SHALL avoid
visual complexity.

---

# GLASSMORPHISM

Glass effects MAY be used
only where they improve hierarchy.

Heavy blur is prohibited.

Transparency SHALL NEVER
reduce readability.

---

# DARK MODE

Dark Mode is the default experience.

Every component SHALL support:

- Dark
- Light

Additional themes MAY be added later.

---

# RESPONSIVENESS

Layouts SHALL support:

- Mobile
- Tablet
- Desktop
- Ultra-wide

Responsive behavior SHALL be
defined before implementation.

---

# ACCESSIBILITY

Color SHALL NEVER be the only
indicator of meaning.

Every interactive element SHALL support:

- Keyboard navigation
- Screen readers
- Focus indicators

Motion SHALL respect
prefers-reduced-motion.

---

# FEEDBACK

Every user action SHALL produce feedback.

Feedback types include:

- Success
- Information
- Warning
- Error
- Loading

Silent interactions are prohibited.

---

# EMPTY STATES

Every empty state SHALL explain:

- Why it is empty
- What the user can do next

Blank screens are prohibited.

---

# LOADING STATES

Skeleton loading is preferred.

Spinners SHALL be used
only when progress cannot
be estimated.

---

# ERROR STATES

Errors SHALL include:

- What happened
- Why
- Suggested action

Technical jargon SHALL be avoided
unless Developer Mode is enabled.

---

# CONSTITUTIONAL DATA

Constitutional information SHALL receive
special visual treatment.

Recommended indicators:

- Gold accents
- Verification badge
- Constitutional status icon

This visually distinguishes protocol
truth from ordinary application data.

---

# CONSISTENCY

Explorer

Wallet

Studio

Governance

Developer Portal

All SHALL appear as members
of one unified ecosystem.

Users SHALL recognize the platform
immediately regardless of product.

---

# EVOLUTION

The design language MAY evolve.

However:

Visual identity SHALL remain recognizable.

Breaking visual changes require:

- ADR
- Design Review
- Governance Approval
