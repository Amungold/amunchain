# Federation Trust Graph

## Purpose

The federation trust graph models trust relationships between constitutional civilizations.

---

## Graph Properties

Each node represents:
- a sovereign civilization
- a unique genesis hash
- a constitutional identity

Each edge represents:
- federation treaty
- compatibility agreement
- trust delegation

---

## Trust States

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Trusted | Fully federated |
| Restricted | Partial federation |
| Observed | Identity known |
| Revoked | Trust revoked |
| Foreign | No trust relationship |

---

## Trust Derivation

Trust derives from:
- genesis lineage
- constitutional signatures
- treaty continuity
- replay equivalence guarantees

---

## Revocation Propagation

Revoked trust propagates through dependent treaties.

---

## Invariant

Trust relationships must remain replayable and verifiable.
