# Federation Handshake Protocol

## Purpose

Defines the first constitutional interaction between two civilizations. The handshake establishes:
- identity
- lineage
- sovereignty
- compatibility
- replay boundaries

---

## Handshake Sequence

### Step 1 - Identity Advertisement
Civilization A sends:
- civilization identifier
- genesis hash
- specification hash
- constitutional version
- federation authority signature

### Step 2 - Identity Verification
Civilization B verifies:
- genesis lineage
- signature validity
- specification integrity
- replay capability

### Step 3 - Reciprocal Advertisement
Civilization B responds with equivalent constitutional identity data.

### Step 4 - Compatibility Evaluation
Both civilizations independently determine:
- semantic compatibility
- replay compatibility
- treaty compatibility
- trust eligibility

### Step 5 - Boundary Negotiation
Replay boundaries and sovereignty limits are established.

### Step 6 - Federation Certificate
If compatibility succeeds:
- federation certificate is issued
- treaty registry is updated
- federation state becomes active

---

## Handshake Failure

Handshake fails if:
- signatures are invalid
- lineage is broken
- replay semantics diverge
- treaties are incompatible
- sovereignty rules conflict

Failed handshakes must be replayable and auditable.

---

## Invariant

No civilization may federate without constitutional verification.
