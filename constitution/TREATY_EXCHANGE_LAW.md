# Treaty Exchange Law

## Principle

Treaties define the constitutional rules of inter-civilization interaction. Treaties are sovereign constitutional artifacts.

---

## Treaty Structure

Every treaty contains:
- treaty identifier
- participating civilizations
- federation scope
- replay boundaries
- compatibility guarantees
- revocation conditions
- cryptographic signatures

---

## Treaty Categories

### Compatibility Treaty
Defines compatible constitutional semantics.

### Replay Treaty
Defines replay interoperability guarantees.

### Trust Treaty
Defines bilateral trust semantics.

### Resource Treaty
Defines resource exchange rules.

### Governance Treaty
Defines inter-civilization governance interactions.

---

## Treaty Validation

A treaty is valid only if:
- all participating civilizations sign
- lineage verification succeeds
- replay guarantees remain intact
- sovereignty boundaries are preserved

---

## Treaty Revocation

Treaties may be revoked through:
- bilateral revocation
- constitutional amendment
- trust failure
- lineage discontinuity

Revocation itself is a constitutional artifact.

---

## Invariant

No treaty may violate frozen constitutional properties.
