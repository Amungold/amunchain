# Constitutional Trust Law

## Trust Model

Constitutional trust is cryptographically derived.
It does not depend on social consensus or external authority.

---

## Trust Derivation

### Origin Trust
Derived from genesis hash. All trust flows from genesis.

### Signature Trust
Derived from constitutional signatures. Valid signatures = valid authority.

### Lineage Trust
Derived from continuous replayable lineage. Broken lineage = broken trust.

### Federation Trust
Derived from bilateral treaty signatures. Requires both civilizations to sign.

---

## Trust Revocation

Trust may be revoked through:
- Signed revocation certificate
- Quorum vote
- Constitutional amendment

Revocation is itself a constitutional artifact.

---

## Trust Inheritance

When authority rotates, trust inherits to new authority through:
- Signed rotation certificate
- Quorum authorization
- Specification hash continuity

---

## Trust Invariants

- All trust derives from genesis
- Broken lineage voids all derived trust
- Revocation is explicit and recorded
- Inheritance requires quorum authorization
