# Federation Certificate Format v1

## Purpose

Federation certificates prove constitutional interoperability between sovereign civilizations.

---

## Certificate Fields

Every federation certificate contains:
1. Civilization A Identifier
2. Civilization B Identifier
3. Genesis Hashes
4. Specification Hashes
5. Treaty Identifiers
6. Compatibility Level
7. Replay Boundary Definition
8. Sovereignty Constraints
9. Timestamp
10. Federation Authority Signatures

---

## Verification Rules

A federation certificate is valid only if:
- all signatures verify
- treaties are active
- lineage continuity exists
- replay boundaries are valid
- sovereignty rules are preserved

---

## Revocation

Certificates may be revoked by:
- treaty revocation
- constitutional incompatibility
- replay divergence
- authority compromise

---

## Invariant

Federation certificates never override constitutional sovereignty.
