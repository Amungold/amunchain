# Replay Boundary Semantics

## Principle

Replay boundaries isolate constitutional execution domains. Federation must never contaminate replay determinism.

---

## Replay Isolation

Each civilization replays:
- its own execution
- its own treaties
- its own constitutional state

Cross-civilization replay is treaty-scoped.

---

## Boundary Rules

Replay boundaries define:
- imported state visibility
- treaty execution scope
- foreign proof admissibility
- replay synchronization rules

---

## Boundary Violations

Violations include:
- unauthorized replay mutation
- foreign replay contamination
- incompatible state injection

Violations trigger federation suspension.

---

## Invariant

Replay determinism is sovereign.
