# Sovereign Boundary Rules

## Principle

Every civilization maintains sovereign constitutional boundaries. Federation does not dissolve sovereignty.

---

## Sovereign Boundaries

Boundaries define:
- replay isolation
- authority isolation
- governance isolation
- storage isolation
- semantic isolation

---

## Boundary Enforcement

Civilizations must reject:
- unauthorized state mutation
- foreign authority escalation
- replay contamination
- treaty violations
- lineage forgery

---

## Boundary Crossing

Boundary crossing is allowed only through:
- signed treaties
- verified federation certificates
- replay-safe protocols

---

## Boundary Failure

A boundary failure occurs when:
- replay determinism breaks
- unauthorized authority appears
- constitutional invariants are violated

Boundary failures trigger federation suspension.

---

## Invariant

No civilization may compromise sovereignty through federation.
