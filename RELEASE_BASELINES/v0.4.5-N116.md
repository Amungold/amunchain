Overview
--------
N115 + N116 complete the cryptographic signing architecture for
SlashingCertificates.

N115 enforces that no unsigned or tampered certificate can reach
the staking execution path. Every slash requires a valid Ed25519
signature over the certificate's canonical bytes.

N116 prevents a single validator from unilaterally producing a
final slashing certificate. Instead, certificates require M-of-N
signatures from the validator set, with quorum verification.
