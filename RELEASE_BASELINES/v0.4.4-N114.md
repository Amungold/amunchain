Overview
--------
N114 adds cryptographic signatures to SlashingCertificates.
Before N114, certificates were verifiable data structures but
carried no proof of who produced them.  Now every certificate
can be signed with an Ed25519 key and verified by any node.

What is included
----------------

N114.2   signing_bytes(), sign(), verify_signature() methods
N114.3   6 signature tests covering sign, verify, tamper detection,
         wrong-key rejection, roundtrip, and content-binding
