Overview
--------
N119 adds a deterministic slashing ledger with replay protection.
Every SlashingCertificate gets a unique certificate_id computed
from its canonical fields via blake3.  The SlashingLedger prevents
the same certificate from being executed more than once and
maintains an audit trail of all executed slashes.

What is included
----------------
N119.1   SlashingLedger with HashSet-based replay protection
N119.2   execute() wraps execution with replay rejection
N119.3   certificate_id() — deterministic, content-based ID
N119.4   Different certificates produce different IDs
N119.5   ExecutedSlash audit trail with validator filtering
